# **Methodology — Calculations & References** 

**The figures used are simplified, rounded, publicly known averages for educational purposes. They are not precise scientific measurements.** 

**However, Peer-reviewed literature supports them as conservative, educationally valid estimates** . 

## **Calculation Model** 

```
Per-Item Impact = Count × Average per Unit
Total Impact    = Sum across all categories
Yearly CO₂ (kg) = Daily CO₂ (g) × 365 ÷ 1000
```
